# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
Everyone is welcome to take part in this project, regardless of background or experience. We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may block participants who violate it. Report problems to sche-max@web.de.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
